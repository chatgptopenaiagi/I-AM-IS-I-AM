# Repository Restructure

- Date: 2026-08-09
- Status: historical and governance record

## Question

How should the repository preserve its prior scientific and historical material while keeping the root README concise, navigable, and explicit about QFP's developing status?

## Purpose

The restructuring establishes a concise repository entrance, a directory architecture for future work, binding claim-status governance, and explicit locations for validation, research history, software, and archived formulations.

## Previous formulation

The previously committed root `README.md` was a long-form repository overview. It combined scientific status, methodology, the Continuum-Projection Ontology, protected gravitational working equations, validation requirements, repository plans, open problems, roadmap material, and project history in one document.

That complete committed README is preserved faithfully at `ARCHIVE/early_repository_material/README_pre_restructure.md`.

## Proposed formulation

The root `README.md` is now a concise entrance and repository map. Binding governance is held in `AGENTS.md`; focused directories provide locations for future foundation, theory, mathematics, models, hypotheses, simulations, experiments, figures, papers, references, research logs, validation, software, and archival work.

## What was retained

The root README retains QFP's developing and non-established scientific status, the claim-status vocabulary, the Continuum-Projection research schemas, the protected scalar-tensor-Gauss-Bonnet action, the static spherical metric, the regular-center expansions, the working central Gauss-Bonnet expression and de Sitter substitution, the schematic central scalar relation, the independence of `F(Phi)` and `H(Phi)`, and the validation principle.

## What was condensed

Long-form methodology, directory plans, validation explanations, reproducibility guidance, open questions, roadmap items, licensing notes, and project-positioning text were condensed into governance rules, the repository map, and concise placeholder documents. Future migration of useful material into focused documents must be deliberate and must retain appropriate claim status and provenance.

## What was moved to the archive

The complete previous committed README was moved, without rewriting its content, to `ARCHIVE/early_repository_material/README_pre_restructure.md`. Archiving it does not mean that any scientific claim in it is accepted, established, experimentally supported, or validated. It is preserved as intellectual history and provenance.

## Reason for change

A shorter root README makes the repository's scientific status, protected working formulations, navigation, and immediate research stage easier to inspect. Separating governance and future subject-specific work reduces the risk that concepts, derivations, numerical results, and empirical evidence become conflated in a single overview.

## Protected-equation confirmation

The restructuring did not intentionally change the mathematical meaning of the protected QFP action, metric, definition of `N(r)`, regular-center expansions, central Gauss-Bonnet expression, de Sitter substitution, or schematic central scalar relation. It also retained the independence of `F(Phi)` and `H(Phi)`, the `C -> Sigma` research schema, and the `Observer in Sigma` concept.

## Claim-status changes introduced by the audit

The central Gauss-Bonnet expression and associated de Sitter substitution are now explicitly labeled **CONJECTURE** because no complete inspectable derivation is presently included in the repository. They are described as current working results awaiting independent derivation and verification.

The central scalar relation is explicitly labeled **CONJECTURE**, with the separate equation qualifier **schematic working relation**. It is not established until checked against the complete field equations.

These labels constrain presentation; they do not alter the equations or supply evidence for them.

The governance taxonomy was subsequently refined to separate scientific object types from epistemic claim statuses. The action is labeled **WORKING MODEL**; coupling independence is an **ASSUMPTION**; the static spherical metric and regular-center series are **ANSATZ** objects; `N(r)` is a **DEFINITION**; and the Continuum-Projection representations are **SCHEMA** objects. These object-type labels describe each item's role and do not strengthen or weaken any scientific claim.

## Scientific consequences

No new empirical support, observation, proof, or completed derivation results from this restructure. The scientific effect is stricter epistemic labeling, clearer provenance, and preservation of the prior repository narrative for later critical migration.
