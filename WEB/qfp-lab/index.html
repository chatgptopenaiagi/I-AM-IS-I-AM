A projection is not a slice.

Start with the solid unit ball x² + y² + z² ≤ 1. Move the cutting plane. Compare the two operations.

ρ(s) = √(1 − s²), |s| ≤ 1

Projection discards z: (0.3, 0.2, +0.5) and (0.3, 0.2, −0.5) share the same image. The orthographic projection stays the unit disk.

This is a Euclidean teaching example, not the author’s ontological Continuum or physical time. A rectangular dimension-reducing map cannot be squared as P² = P.
