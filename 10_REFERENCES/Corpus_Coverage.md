## Local investigation

Two existing engineering inventories were filtered for project-relevant names and paths; neither contained an I-AM/QFP match. A bounded metadata search inspected **372 directories and 7,630 entries**, to depth two beneath twelve likely project/download/personal-document roots. The search finished with no enumeration errors or queued directories remaining inside those bounds.

The selected locations covered current project/AI workspace and download roots on the accessible engineering drive, plus the user's Downloads, Documents, Desktop and OneDrive roots. Reparse points, dependency/cache directories, virtual environments and build output trees were excluded. Searches targeted the project name, QFP, Quantum Folding, ChatGPT/export markers, manuscript/research-pack names, `I_AM_Visual_Laboratory.html` and `import_chatgpt_archive.py`.

No relevant local export, named preview/importer, research pack or alternate old checkout was found within those bounds. The only ChatGPT filename hit was an installer. The currently used checkout was freshly cloned for this mission, so it is not presented as a recovered historical checkout. The historical QFP directory supplied in the mission was absent at observation time.

This was **not** a whole-disk recursive scan. Deep unnamed archives, unrelated private files, dependency trees and arbitrary ZIP contents were not examined. “Not found within coverage” does not mean absent everywhere.

## Connected-source investigation

Targeted Drive searches used QFP, Quantum Folding and continuum, followed by bounded related-term searches. Only project-relevant candidate documents were opened. Search metadata was used to distinguish native documents from DOCX files and to ground the selected source IDs before reads.

A Pages search performed by the integration agent returned no relevant research source. No unsupported complete ChatGPT-history API was assumed. A search result or private document title was not treated as access to the owner's complete chat history.

Readable native-document and DOCX extraction can omit equation objects, layout or other content. The gravity manuscript has visible equation gaps; the AGI draft includes broken formula markup. Preserved readable extracts are therefore source evidence with extraction limits, not faithful facsimiles of every original page. Missing formulas were not reconstructed from intuition.

## Publication boundary

The repository contains source summaries, stable redacted source IDs, selected equations and review annotations. It contains no full private conversation dump, private Drive URL, native document ID, account identifier or unrelated personal file content. Private source lookup metadata and extracts are stored outside the repository.

The canonical registry is unchanged. Newly curated historical records and present review records do not silently become canonical objects or gain a stronger epistemic status.
