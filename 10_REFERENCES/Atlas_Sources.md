### Brihaye et al. — scalar coupling to Ricci and Gauss–Bonnet curvature

Yves Brihaye, Betti Hartmann, Nathália Pio Aprile and Jon Urrestilla, *Scalarization of asymptotically anti–de Sitter black holes with applications to holographic phase transitions*, **Physical Review D 101, 124016** (11 June 2020), [doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.101.124016](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.101.124016); [author manuscript, arXiv:1911.01950v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.01950v2).

**Precise use:** A concrete published model containing scalar couplings to both Ricci and Gauss–Bonnet curvature, studied for static, spherical, asymptotically AdS black holes. It motivates comparison of specified couplings, boundary conditions and scalarization calculations. Its restricted couplings, electromagnetic sector and asymptotics must not be substituted for QFP's independent general functions or regular-center assumptions. It supplies no proof of QFP regularity or ontology. **Inspected:** abstract and manuscript introduction/model discussion, pp. 1–3; no equation is imported here.

### Kleihaus, Kunz and Kanti — distinct notions of regularity

Burkhard Kleihaus, Jutta Kunz and Panagiota Kanti, *Properties of ultracompact particlelike solutions in Einstein-scalar-Gauss-Bonnet theories*, **Physical Review D 102, 024070** (23 July 2020), [doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.102.024070](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.102.024070).

**Precise use:** The published abstract distinguishes a globally regular metric and nonpathological effective stress-energy from a scalar field that diverges at the origin. This is a direct reason to specify which field or invariant “regular” describes. Those particlelike solutions are not the same assumption as QFP's smooth, finite scalar center expansion. Their reported behavior is not evidence that QFP has a global solution. **Inspected:** publisher bibliographic record and complete abstract; no claim of a full-paper equation audit.

### David Tong — string theory

### BIPM — kelvin is a thermodynamic-temperature unit

Bureau International des Poids et Mesures, [*SI base unit: kelvin (K)*](https://www.bipm.org/en/web/guest/si-base-units/kelvin), current official definition page.

**Precise use:** The kelvin measures thermodynamic temperature and is defined through the fixed Boltzmann constant. This grounds the distinction between temperature and the laboratory's dimensionless logarithmic coordinate. A chosen coordinate origin is not thereby zero kelvin, vacuum, zero energy or an ontological ground. **Inspected:** complete definition page; no thermodynamic or vacuum claim is inferred from the atlas's scale slider.

### National Academies — reproducibility and replicability

National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, *Reproducibility and Replicability in Science* (Washington, DC: National Academies Press, 2019), [doi:10.17226/25303](https://doi.org/10.17226/25303), [Summary, definitions and reporting recommendations](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/25303/chapter/3).

**Precise use:** The report's terminology distinguishes rerunning computations with the original inputs and methods from independent studies collecting their own data. Its reporting guidance supports recording code, environment, inputs and uncertainty. Accordingly, passing symbolic or browser tests is computational evidence, not independent experimental replication or empirical confirmation of QFP. **Inspected:** Summary, pp. 5–8, including definitions and Recommendation 4-1. The report acknowledges that terminology varies across disciplines.
